How it works

Sharing takes less than a minute — and it's always free.

  1. 01

    Post your item

    Describe it, choose a category and set a pickup spot near you.

  2. 02

    Get a request

    Neighbours who need it send a request. You decide who receives it.

  3. 03

    Meet and share

    Hand it over at a public pickup spot. No fees, no selling — just sharing.

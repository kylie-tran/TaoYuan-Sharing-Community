    🌿 About Taoyuan Sharing

    Taoyuan Sharing Old Stuffs is a local community built to encourage people to give new life to old items. Instead of throwing things away, we connect donors with those who truly need them — creating a cycle of kindness across Taoyuan.

    🌸 Our Mission

    To build a sustainable and caring society where sharing replaces waste, and every unused item finds a new home filled with gratitude.

    💬 Q&A

    Who can join the community?

    Anyone living in Taoyuan who wants to share or receive old items.

    How do I donate?

    Simply register and list your available items. We’ll match you with someone in need.
